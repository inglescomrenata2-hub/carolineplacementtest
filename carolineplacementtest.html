Alex and the 15 Extra Minutes

Alex is a product designer at a software company. Last year, Alex used to spend about twenty minutes every morning choosing an outfit. "It was silly," Alex says. "I was already tired before my first meeting."

In January, Alex decided to change this. Since then, Alex has built a small capsule wardrobe with a handful of versatile pieces: black jeans, two plain shirts, a blazer and one pair of comfortable sneakers. Alex has also donated about forty items that never fit well.

Last week, Alex was getting dressed for an important demo when the manager called and moved the meeting one hour earlier. Because the outfit was already simple, Alex was ready in five minutes and arrived on time. "That was the moment I knew it worked," Alex says. There is still one drawer to declutter, but Alex has already saved around fifteen minutes every morning.
